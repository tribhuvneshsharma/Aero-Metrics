# Judge Demo Script & Flow (90 Seconds)

## 1. Scenario
"A policy analyst or consumer monitoring airfares observes surging prices on the Delhi-Mumbai (DEL-BOM) corridor one week prior to travel. How does APIx capture, validate, and incorporate this without skewing national CPI metrics?"

## 2. Presentation Walkthrough
1. **Executive Overview**: Show headline APIx, 1-day/7-day/30-day changes, weighted coverage (e.g. 98.2%), and active data mode (`replay_fixture`).
2. **Route Heatmap**: Identify where price pressure originates across the 16 domestic directional corridors.
3. **Lead-Time Dynamics**: Compare T+1 surge vs. stable T+30 booking horizon fares.
4. **Data Quality & Traceability**: Click any data point to inspect raw quotes, cleaning decisions, imputation logs, and quality scores.
5. **API & Open Standards**: Showcase OpenAPI documentation for RBI / NSO consumption.
